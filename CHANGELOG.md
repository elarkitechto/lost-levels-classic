# Releases

## 1.1.0

- Maze checkpoints play the coin chime for a correct route and the fireworks sound for a wrong one.
- Jumping into the underside of a castle firebar pivot stops the jump and prevents damage from that bar on the contact frame.

## 1.0.0

Convert the Japanese FDS Lost Levels to NES, with SMB1-style scenery, platforms, mushrooms, bricks and castles. Includes the final scoped bridge and Blooper palette fixes. Original Mario and Luigi physics are preserved.

One final enhancement profile.
